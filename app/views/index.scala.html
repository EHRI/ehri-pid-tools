EHRI Persistent Identifiers

This service displays metadata about articles and other outputs created by the European Holocaust Research Infrastructure (EHRI) that have been assigned persistent identifiers (PIDs for short). EHRI assigns different kinds of PID depending on the type of resource:

EHRI uses DataCite to register Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) for publications and datasets.

EHRI's online portal uses Archival Resource Keys (ARKs) for metadata and other information resources.

ORCID iDs identify researchers and their contributions to EHRI outputs.

EHRI is registered with the research organization registry as ror:00zsk2t64.

The EHRI Document Blog, as a serial publication, carries the e-ISSN 3118-0191.

See the @Messages("pid.policy") for more information, including details of versioning, termination, and assignment of responsibilities.
